The idea

A safe harbour for everything under pressure.

Many operators wait weeks for safety valves, pressure regulators and gauges to be tested. Large testing companies are often far away.

My approach: local, fast, properly documented.

Precise

Calibrated references, traceable reports

Fast

Short turnaround instead of long waiting lists

Personal

Planned services

Testing & service. From one source.

01

Safety valves

Set-pressure and leak-tightness testing on the test bench, adjustment, with test report.

02

Pressure regulators & safety shut-off valves

Repair, readjustment and function and leak testing of gas pressure regulators and safety shut-off valves.

03

Pressure gauges

Comparison against a calibrated reference, documentation.

04

GMP diaphragm valves

Diaphragm replacement for pharma and biotech, documented to customer requirements.

05

LPG in leisure vehicles

Gas inspection to DVGW G 607 for motorhomes and caravans.

06

Gas installations & compressed air

Periodic and extraordinary inspections of LPG (propane/butane) and compressed-air systems. Natural gas systems in cooperation with a registered specialist contractor.1

1 Services being set up. Their scope depends on the permits granted (exemption under §8 HwO, registration with the gas network operator).
